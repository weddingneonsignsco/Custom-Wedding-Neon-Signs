Frequently Asked Questions About Custom Wedding Neon Signs
What can I put on a custom wedding neon sign?

You can personalize your sign with names, initials, a last name, wedding dates, hashtags, quotes, phrases, or other custom wording. The available customization depends on the specific design and sign size.

How do I choose the right wedding neon sign size?

Consider where you plan to display the sign and how far away guests and cameras will be. Larger signs are often better for wedding backdrops and reception spaces, while smaller designs can work well on tables and smaller decorative areas.

Where should I put my wedding neon sign?

Popular locations include wedding backdrops, sweetheart tables, reception areas, photo booths, cake tables, dessert tables, bars, and dance floors.

Can I use my wedding neon sign after the wedding?

Yes. A custom wedding neon sign can become a keepsake and be displayed in your home or used for future celebrations.

Are custom wedding neon signs good for photo booths?

Yes. Personalized neon signs can create an attractive focal point for wedding photo booths and guest photos. Your names, wedding date, hashtag, or favorite phrase can make the photo area feel more personal.

Can I customize the color and font?

Customization options depend on the specific sign design. Many custom wedding neon signs can be personalized with different colors, fonts, sizes, and wording.

How far in advance should I order my wedding neon sign?

It's best to order your custom sign well before your wedding date so there is enough time for design, production, shipping, and any unexpected delays. Check the current production and delivery timeframe for the specific sign you're ordering.
